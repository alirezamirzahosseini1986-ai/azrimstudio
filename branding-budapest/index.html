Brand Identity & Logo Design in Budapest

Magyarul

A logo is a picture. A brand is a system your customers recognise everywhere — on the sign, the menu, the website and Instagram. We design both, so every new piece looks like it belongs to you.

What you get

  • Short strategy workshop — who you serve, what makes you different, how you want to sound.
  • Logo family — main logo, a simplified mark for small sizes (profile pictures, favicons) and a one-colour version.
  • Colour and type system — exact colour codes for screen and print, and fonts with clear rules for use.
  • Brand guidelines — a practical document your team, printer and web developer can follow.
  • First applications — business cards, social media templates, signage or packaging, depending on what you need first.

Who it is for

Cafés and restaurants, dental and medical clinics, shops and fashion labels, schools and service businesses that want to look as good as the work they do — whether you are opening, rebranding, or entering a new market.

How we work

1. Listen — a call or meeting in Budapest to understand your business and customers. 2. Direction — two or three clearly different concepts. 3. Refine — we develop the chosen direction with your feedback. 4. Deliver — all files in the formats you need, plus the guidelines.

Work we have done

Recent design, branding and social media projects include Violindent and Rad Dent (dental clinics), Arádi Market (supermarket), Turanna Fashion, New Way Group and Avicenna International College. You can see them in our portfolio.

Languages

We work in English and Hungarian, and can also prepare materials in Persian and Arabic for brands that speak to international audiences.

Questions

How long does a brand identity take?

Most small-business identities take three to six weeks, depending on how many applications you need and how quickly feedback comes back.

Can you redesign my existing logo instead of starting from zero?

Yes. Often the best result keeps what customers already recognise and fixes what doesn’t work — legibility, colours or consistency.

Do I own the files?

Yes. After final payment you receive the final and source files and an unlimited licence to use them for your business. Third-party fonts or stock images may need their own licence.
