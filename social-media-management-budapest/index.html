Social Media Management in Budapest

Magyarul

Posting every day is easy. Posting things people stop for, save and send to a friend is the work. We plan, design and publish social media that looks like a real brand — not like a template.

What you get

  • Content strategy — your content pillars, posting rhythm and the formats that suit your audience.
  • Designed posts and carousels — built on your brand system, so your feed is recognisable at a glance.
  • Reels and stories — short-form video ideas with a strong hook, script and call to action.
  • Captions in English and Hungarian — written for your audience, with relevant keywords instead of hashtag spam.
  • Monthly review — what worked, what didn’t, and what we change next month.

Who it is for

Businesses that know social media matters but don’t have the time or the design team to do it well: cafés, restaurants, clinics, shops, schools and local service companies.

How we work

1. Audit — we look at your current profiles and competitors. 2. Plan — a monthly calendar you approve before anything is posted. 3. Create — designs, videos and captions. 4. Publish and review — scheduled posting and a monthly report based on your real Insights data.

Platforms

Instagram, Facebook and TikTok, with LinkedIn for B2B companies.

Questions

Do I need to post every day?

Not necessarily. Consistency matters more than volume — three strong posts a week usually beat seven rushed ones.

Will you reply to comments and messages?

We can include community management in the plan, or set up a simple routine your team can follow.

Do you run paid ads?

Yes, we can plan and run Meta ads alongside organic content. The advertising budget is paid directly to the platform.
