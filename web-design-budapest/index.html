Website Design for Small Businesses in Budapest

Magyarul

Your website has one job: help the right visitor understand what you do and take the next step — call, book, order or visit. We design sites that are fast on a phone, clear in the first five seconds and easy for you to keep up to date.

What you get

  • Structure and messaging — one clear message per page and an obvious next step.
  • Design that matches your brand — not a theme that a thousand other businesses also use.
  • Mobile-first build — fast loading, light images and clean code.
  • Multilingual pages — English and Hungarian, with other languages on request, set up correctly for search engines.
  • Search basics — page titles, descriptions, structured data, sitemap, and help connecting Google Search Console and your Google Business Profile.

Who it is for

Clinics, restaurants, shops, schools and service companies that need a professional site without an oversized budget or a complicated system to maintain.

How we work

1. Plan — pages, content and goals. 2. Design — the key pages for your approval. 3. Build — the full site, tested on phones and computers. 4. Launch — domain, analytics and search setup, plus a short handover.

Recent work

Examples include the Rad Dent clinic website and our own multilingual studio site. More projects are in our portfolio.

Questions

Can I edit the website myself?

Yes. We choose the setup based on how often you need to change things, and show you how to update the essentials.

Will my website appear on Google?

We set up the technical foundations correctly. Ranking also depends on your content, reviews and competition, so we will give you a clear plan rather than a guarantee.

Do you also host the website?

We can recommend and set up reliable hosting, or work with what you already have.
